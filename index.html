About these rates

UtilityCheck is an independent, informational project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ERC, NEA, PRESYO-PH or any utility, and company names are used only to identify them.

Electric rates are indicative monthly residential averages in ₱/kWh, mostly from PRESYO-PH (ICSC), along with official advisories and community reports where labeled. They are not a utility bill. Actual charges vary by month, consumption tier and customer class, and your utility's official bill always prevails.

Water figures come from a water-rates database of water district tariffs. The headline price is the monthly bill at 20 m³, computed from each district's published rate tiers. Many districts have not updated their data recently (marked “Outdated”), and water district pins are placed at the district's city or at province level where marked “approximate”.

We try to keep the data accurate and current, but we can't guarantee it is complete, correct or up to date, and it should not be your only basis for a financial decision.
